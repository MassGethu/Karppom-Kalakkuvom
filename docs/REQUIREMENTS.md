# PS3 compliance matrix

Source: official *Problem Statement 3 | Education — The Last-Mile Tutor*, p. 1, supplied with the project. Status for every row is planned. P0 is a release gate. The brief names one state-board subject and asks for useful operation in poor conditions, including progress preservation. A single topic, airplane-mode restart, real reconnection sync and persistent misconception memory are project choices that make the prototype verifiable. The PDF does not require offline generation, full-subject coverage, both voice and photo, or a remote adult portal.

| ID | Official requirement or guardrail | Planned feature | Priority and acceptance | Demo or audit evidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| R1 | One specific target, one regional language, one class and one subject | Tamil-medium Class 8 mathematics learner with a basic shared Android phone | P0 · Only the stated persona and subject in onboarding and learning pack | Show target and open the module on Android |
| R2 | Regional language end to end across interface, explanation and practice | Tamil-first UI, written examples, tap controls, feedback, online tutor, progress and fallback states | P0 · Reviewer signs off the normal and error flows | Walk both learner and adult paths, including outage and photo errors |
| R3 | Real state-board syllabus for the class and subject | Algebra, §3.8.4 one-variable linear-equation solutions and §3.8 prerequisites | P0 · Every shipped item maps to a syllabus objective and has verified math | Show state-published section, mapping and original exercise |
| R4 | AI tutor explains in simple local language and answers doubts | Online grounded Tamil hints and explanations | P0 · Real request, validated supported math, reviewed language, useful fallback on outage | Ask a novel in-scope text doubt and an actual photo doubt |

| G1 | Minors and minimum personal data | Synthetic IDs and aliases, limited events, no contact info or advertising trackers, photo safeguards | P0 · Review request bodies, persistence and logs | Inspect schema and storage, explain crop and deletion policy |
| G2 | Test only with sample learner profiles | Three synthetic profiles and synthetic algebra photos | P0 · Fixtures and test accounts contain fictional data only | Identify fixtures in test and demo setup |
| G3 | Openly available curriculum and no textbook copying | Cite the published reference, author examples and visuals independently, maintain content provenance | P0 · Every shipped item has curriculum mapping and original provenance | Audit pack manifest and inspect one independently written example |
| Q1 | Who supports the product when learners cannot pay? | NGO or CSR pilot with school or community partners | Presentation · Explain sponsor and cost assumptions | Include the sustainability hypothesis in the pitch |

The PDF's three design questions cover typing, shared devices/data loss and sustainability. The first two are addressed by R6–R8. Q1 captures the funding discussion.
