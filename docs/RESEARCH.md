# Discovery and product assessment

## Recommendation

Retain Tamil, Class 8, Tamil Nadu State Board and Mathematics. Teach one-variable linear-equation solving. Equality preservation and inverse operations give the learner choices that reveal distinct errors. Substitution allows an independent correctness check. The scope supports a short Tamil lesson, a structural progression, a targeted intervention and a clean handwritten photo demo. Fraction arithmetic offers rich misconceptions too, while algebra gives an especially direct diagnostic step and a verified Class 8 section. The [PRD](PRD.md) identifies the curriculum anchor and edition caveat.

## Learner evidence

- Rural Tamil Nadu ASER 2024 reports a smartphone at home for 92.2% of surveyed 14–16-year-olds. Among those who could use a smartphone, 32.7% reported owning one. Among surveyed Class VIII children, 40.0% could perform the ASER division task, including 37.8% of government-school children. The access group and class measure are different populations. Neither statistic establishes usable bandwidth, Class VIII phone ownership or the algebra ability of an individual. Build for interruption and check arithmetic prerequisites. [ASER 2024 Rural Tamil Nadu, tables 7, 9 and 10](https://asercentre.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/12/Tamil-Nadu-1.pdf)
- UNESCO documents the value of education in a language learners understand and the difficulty of resourcing multilingual education. Design Tamil copy with learners' school vocabulary and original familiar contexts. Short sessions, Android constraints and adult availability are persona assumptions for later validation. [UNESCO report on languages in Indian education](https://unesdoc.unesco.org/ark:/48223/pf0000397158)

## Instructional choices

| Research | Implementation implication |
| --- | --- |
| Formative assessment uses evidence to change teaching. | Select the next step, then assess whether the correction helped. [Black and Wiliam](https://ditc.ccny.cuny.edu/docs/blackBox.pdf) |
| Mastery learning specifies competencies and allows corrective teaching and different amounts of practice. | Model explicit prerequisite competencies and collect more than one independent observation before marking a skill demonstrated. [Bloom](https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED053419) |
| Intelligent tutoring has long used explicit rules for plausible math errors. | Prefer observable operations over guesses from the final value, and ask a probe if multiple explanations fit. [Koedinger and Corbett](https://pact.cs.cmu.edu/koedinger/pubs/Intelligent%20Tutors.pdf) |
| Mixed mathematics practice can support later strategy selection. Spacing effectiveness depends on the retention horizon. | Use targeted checks immediately after repair and mix skills in a later revisit. Evidence and scheduling thresholds remain prototype heuristics. [Rohrer et al.](https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000367) · [Cepeda et al.](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1467-9280.2008.02209.x) |

## Curriculum verification and language review

The 2022-revised Government of Tamil Nadu Tamil-medium Class 8 textbook covers one-variable equations in Algebra §3.8. Its solution section teaches inverse operations and illustrates preserving both sides, with substitution to check solutions. Cite the specific edition, chapter, section and printed pages in the pack metadata. The source was publicly readable via a mirror, and the SCERT taxonomy lists Class 8 Mathematics. The current 2026–27 classroom edition was not independently confirmed. Have a Tamil-medium math teacher check the active school edition and review the new lesson independently. Author all explanatory prose, diagrams, contexts and exercises from scratch. Public access establishes a curriculum reference. Reusable license and copyright clearance are separate questions. [SCERT class listing](https://tnschools.gov.in/scert-taxonomy/class-8-maths) · [Tamil government textbook, mirrored](https://dl.tamilbookspdf.com/TN%20Text%20Books/Tamil%208th%20Class%20Books/Std_08_All_Subjects/8th_Mathematics_TM%20-%20www.TamilBooksPDF.com.pdf) · [Academic archive record for the board's 2024 English edition](https://schoolbooksarchive.azimpremjiuniversity.edu.in/handle/20.500.12497/11958)
